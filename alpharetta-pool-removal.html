Pool Removal Options for Alpharetta Homeowners

Removing an unused swimming pool can create more usable yard space. The best approach depends on the pool shell, access for equipment, drainage, and how you plan to use the property afterward.

Full Pool Removal

The shell is removed, debris is hauled away, and the area is backfilled and graded. If you plan to build on the site, discuss engineering and testing requirements before work starts.

Partial Removal or Fill-In

Part of the shell may remain below grade, subject to applicable rules. This can affect future construction and property disclosures, so confirm the scope and limitations in writing.

What Does Pool Removal Cost in Alpharetta?

Price depends on pool dimensions and material, full versus partial removal, machinery access, concrete disposal, imported fill, grading, and any required inspections. Narrow side yards, slopes, retaining walls, or extensive landscaping can increase complexity.

Permits, HOA Rules and Property Access

Before demolition, confirm which city or county office has jurisdiction over your parcel and whether permits or inspections are required. HOA or architectural review requirements may apply separately from municipal permits; HOA approval does not replace government approval.

Ask the contractor how equipment will reach the pool, whether fences or paving need temporary protection, where debris will be loaded, and how stormwater runoff will be managed.

For more background, visit the Metro Atlanta permits guide and Fulton County pool removal guide.

Questions to Ask Before Hiring

  • Is full or partial removal appropriate for my future plans?
  • Who will check permits and HOA approvals?
  • What is included in debris hauling, backfill and grading?
  • How will the driveway, landscaping and fencing be protected?
  • Will the contractor provide insurance details and any required inspection records?

Alpharetta Pool Removal FAQs

Can I build over a filled-in pool?

Do not assume a filled-in pool area is suitable for construction. The required removal method, engineered fill, and testing depend on the proposed structure and applicable approvals.

Do I need HOA approval?

Possibly. Review your association's rules and confirm any required architectural approval before scheduling work.

How long does pool demolition take?

Duration varies with size, material, access, weather, hauling, and permit or inspection timing. Request a written schedule for your specific project.
