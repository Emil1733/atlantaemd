Swimming Pool Removal Options in Dunwoody

For homeowners replacing an aging pool with lawn, landscaping, or another outdoor feature, the appropriate removal method depends on the existing shell, yard access, drainage, and future use.

Full Pool Demolition

Complete shell removal generally involves breaking up and hauling away the structure, then backfilling and grading the excavation. If you plan to build on the site, discuss engineered fill and inspection requirements before choosing a scope.

Partial Pool Removal or Fill-In

Some projects may allow part of the shell to remain below grade. Confirm whether that approach is permitted and how it may affect future construction, drainage, or disclosures.

Compare approaches in our pool removal guide and pool demolition guide.

What Affects Pool Removal Cost in Dunwoody?

Pool size, construction material, full versus partial demolition, disposal, imported fill, grading, and equipment access all influence cost. Narrow side yards, mature trees, retaining walls, and steep slopes can complicate removal.

Our Metro Atlanta pool removal cost guide explains general pricing factors. Request a property-specific estimate before making a budget decision.

Permits, Trees and Access Planning

Check the City of Dunwoody's current permit requirements for your address and scope of work. Demolition, grading, erosion control, or tree-related rules may apply; do not assume an approval timeframe before confirming with the city.

Ask the contractor to identify utility lines, plan safe equipment access, discuss tree protection, and explain how stormwater will be managed during excavation and backfill.

See the Metro Atlanta permit guide and DeKalb County pool removal guide for additional context.

Questions to Ask a Pool Demolition Contractor

  • Is full or partial removal appropriate for my plans?
  • Who will check city permits and schedule any inspections?
  • Can equipment access the pool without removing fencing or damaging landscaping?
  • How will debris, fill, drainage and grading be handled?
  • What insurance details and completion documentation will be provided?

Dunwoody Pool Removal FAQs

Does pool demolition in Dunwoody require a permit?

Requirements depend on the property and scope. Confirm directly with the City of Dunwoody before work begins.

Can I build on the area after pool removal?

Future building plans may require complete removal, engineered backfill, testing, and separate permits. Discuss the intended use with the relevant professionals and authorities.

How long will the project take?

Timing depends on pool type, access, weather, debris hauling, and any permit or inspection steps. Ask for a written project schedule.
